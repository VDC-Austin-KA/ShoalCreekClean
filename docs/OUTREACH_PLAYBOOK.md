# Shoal Creek Clean: Outreach Playbook

Companion to `MARKET_STRATEGY.md`. Use these templates only after the pricing engine matches the price book (strategy Section 4.2).

**Merge tags:** `{{first_name}}`, `{{company}}`, `{{city}}`. Fill from the CRM before sending. If `{{first_name}}` is missing, greet with "Hello there." Never send a message with an unfilled tag.

## 1. Cadence and Principles

- Lead with the property, not the product. Name a specific site when research allows.
- One ask per message: a 15-minute call or a site walk, not a contract.
- Every touch mentions photo documentation or the client portal. That is our differentiator.
- Cadence: Day 0 email; Day 2 LinkedIn note; Day 4 call; Day 7 follow-up; Day 10 drop-in (storefronts and restaurants only); Day 60 re-engagement with a seasonal hook (Section 8).
- Log every touch, outcome, and objection in the CRM.

## 8. Seasonal Promotions

Apply every discount through the engine's frequency or promo settings so the quote, invoice, and photo record match. Do not offer anything outside the price book.

**Pollen season (February to April)**
- Hook: pollen and tree debris settle on walkways, lots, and storefronts every spring.
- Offer: book a flatwork or storefront clean before March 31, and enroll in a bi-annual schedule for 8 percent off each visit.
- Subject: "Spring pollen is on your walkways. Book before the next storm."

**Post-summer grime (September to October)**
- Hook: summer heat and humidity leave mildew and grime on building exteriors.
- Offer: book building soft wash or gutter cleaning in September or October, and enroll in a quarterly schedule (15 percent frequency discount).
- Subject: "Clean the building before winter, not after."

**Holiday storefront prep (November)**
- Hook: shoppers judge the storefront before they walk in.
- Offer: book a storefront or sidewalk clean by November 20. Retail centers that book a center-wide clean get priority scheduling.
- Subject: "Get the storefront ready for the holiday rush."

**Year-round**
- Referral: one month of recurring service credit for any referred account that signs.
- Pilot: first service at a reduced rate for three accounts per market, in exchange for a reference and a photo case study. Get written approval for any case-study use.

## 9. Compliance Reminders

- Confirm contact status before any cold email. Honor opt-outs immediately and keep a suppression list.
- Include an opt-out option in every commercial email.
- Do not claim insurance, certifications, or clients the company does not hold at the time of sending.
- Do not use prospect logos or site photos without written permission.
- **Verify** with counsel the Texas rules on unsolicited commercial email and the city rules on door-to-door solicitation.
